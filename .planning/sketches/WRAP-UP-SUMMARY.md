## Design Direction

Autonomica is a systems lab building the operating system for an autonomous company. Preserve the public research-experiment identity while showing products as working modules created by the experiment and services as bounded ways to apply its methods elsewhere.

The selected visual language combines a near-black industrial surface, warm ivory editorial typography, acid-lime system signals, rare orange experimental labels, compact mono metadata, asymmetric layouts, and plausible operating-loop details.

## Key Decisions

- Keep the experiment and autonomous-company operating system as the primary story.
- Sequence the homepage as thesis → method → products → services → field notes → bounded CTA.
- Use Syne for engineered display type, Newsreader italic for a human editorial accent, IBM Plex Sans for copy, and IBM Plex Mono for system language.
- Preserve the exact winning palette in `sources/themes/default.css`.
- Use products as proof, not as an unsupported mature suite.
- Use services as narrow engagements, not as generic AI consulting.
- Favor thin borders, large type, operational diagrams, logs, and status over generic SaaS cards.
- Keep motion quick, quiet, responsive, and tied to system state.
