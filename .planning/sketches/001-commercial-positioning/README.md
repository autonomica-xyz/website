# Sketch 001: Commercial Positioning

## Design Question

Should the expanded Autonomica site present the company primarily as a balanced systems lab, a software product foundry, or an applied AI services partner?

## How to View

Open `.planning/sketches/001-commercial-positioning/index.html` in a browser.

## Variants

- **A: Systems Lab** — balances research, working systems, and scoped service engagements while staying closest to the current identity.
- **B: Product Foundry** — leads with AutoDevOps, GitHub Watch, and GetLate MCP as a coherent software portfolio.
- **C: Applied AI Partner** — leads with outcomes and engagement models, using working systems as proof of execution.

## Selected Direction

**Variant A: Systems Lab**

Autonomica remains a public research experiment whose central project is building the operating system for an autonomous company. Products are working modules created by that effort. Services are ways to apply the same methods to a bounded external workflow. Neither should turn the company into a generic AI software vendor or consultancy.

Preserve the dark industrial/editorial visual system, acid-lime primary accent, warm ivory text, restrained orange signals, system diagrams, operating logs, technical labels, and the research → build → operate narrative.

## Content Assumptions

- “Autonomous websites,” “Research agents,” and “Local models” are the user-selected compact showcase labels.
- Active application building and local-model experiments on constrained hardware were confirmed by the user.
- Distributed transcription orchestration remains a confirmed built system but is no longer a featured Variant A label. Its description is grounded in the local `workqueue` repository and timestamped transcription worker.
- "Operations Audit," "System Build Sprint," and "Managed Autonomy" are proposed service packages, not confirmed offers.
- No performance, customer, or revenue claims are introduced.

## What to Look For

When implementing the real site, keep the Systems Lab identity and research experiment visible before introducing systems and services. System detail can borrow some of Variant B's clarity, but not its portfolio-first positioning. Service detail can borrow Variant C's bounded engagement model, but not its consultancy-first positioning.
