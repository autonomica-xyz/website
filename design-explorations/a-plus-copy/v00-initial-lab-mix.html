01 · The lab

One founder.
Infinite applied AI.

Most companies use AI as a helper inside human workflows. We’re testing what happens when systems own the loop — decide, act, course-correct — and when volume work is engineered for unit economics instead of demos.

Client work is not a side shop. It’s how the lab stays honest: real data shapes, real cost pressure, real failure modes.

Autonomous operations

AI owns execution loops. Humans set direction and handle true edge cases — not every approval.

02

Cost as architecture

For batch work, $/item is a design input: model ladders, distribution, retries, QC sampling.

03

Force multipliers

White-glove embeds find where human time is the bottleneck and install systems that multiply capacity.

04

Publish what works

Findings and patterns leave the lab as blueprints over time — the experiment is meant to transfer.
